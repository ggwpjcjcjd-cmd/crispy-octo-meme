One uninterrupted ten-second fixed high-altitude aerial wide shot of the complete reference valley. Mountains, river bends, town and every existing bridge remain unchanged at their original scale. The only main action is a new small bridge being assembled across the nearest previously empty river channel beside the town. First, separate rigid timber piers are planted at fixed points. Horizontal beams are lifted between the piers one at a time, then short deck sections and parapets are installed successively until the new crossing is connected. Material stacks on the bank shrink as assembly progresses. River water flows around the piers throughout. Tiny workers remain indistinct landscape details. Photoreal documentary time lapse. No cut, close-up, zoom, camera movement, floating bridge, rubber stretching, transparency reveal, dialogue or narration.
